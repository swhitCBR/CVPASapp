
### How are Environmental Variables Incorporated into predictions?

The statistical models that underly the prediction tool related route-use and route-specific survival to the environmental and operational conditions that individual fish are expected to have experienced. Raw environmental and operational data, which is reported on daily and sub-daily timescales, was obtained from online portals such on websites such as (add link) CDEC and then subset and summarized for use as explanatory variables in statistical models. The time of their arrival at a junction (i.e., first detection at HOR and TCJ) served as a starting point for the summarization period in most cases . The length of time over which variables were summarized depended on the response being modeled, with a 24-hour period applied to variables used routing models and a multi-day summarization periods used for survival models. For example, the probability of a fish surviving along the Old River route between HOR and CHP was estimated based on the environmental and operation conditions during a five-day window beginning with the arrival of that individual at HOR.
