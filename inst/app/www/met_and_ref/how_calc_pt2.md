### How are $S_{HOR-CHP}$ and $S_{TCJ-CHP}$ are computed?

Overall survival estimates are generated by combining user-specified inputs (i.e., date of arrival at HOR or TCJ and a set of environmental conditions) with parameter estimates from existing statistical models. For the sake of simplicity, the explanation given here is limited making a prediction for an individual day and ignores explanatory variables. Juvenile Steelhead arriving at the HOR junction take either the San Joaquin River (SJL) or the Old River (ORE) routes at frequencies corresponding to route-usage probability $\Psi_{SJL}$ and $\Psi_{ORE}$. Notably, route usage probabilities at junctions sum to 1, so knowing one of these probabilities is sufficient for calculating the other (e.g., $\Psi_{ORE}=1-\Psi_{SJL}$). There are no major junctions along the ORE between HOR and CHP, so the probability of survival along this path is given by the parameter $\theta_{HOR-CHP\,|\,ORE}$. Fish traveling along the SJL between HOR and TCJ survive at a rate determined by $\theta_{HOR-TCJ\,|\,SJL}$and those that make it to the junction alive are, once again, compelled to one of two possible paths. Fish can remain in the San Joaquin River and travel to CHP via the MacDonald Island route (MAC) at a frequency that corresponds to the route-usage probability $\Psi_{MAC}$ and where they and are expected survive at a rate corresponding to $\theta_{TCJ-CHP\,|\,MAC}$. Remaining migrants take the Turner Cut route (TRN) at a frequency corresponding to $\Psi_{TRN}$ (i.e., $1-\Psi_{MAC}$), where they survive at a rate that corresponds to $\theta_{TCJ-CHP\,|\,TRN}$. The vertical bar (“|”) in the $\theta$ subscripts indicates that the survival probability is conditional on the route that is taken. Estimates of overall survival probability incorporate survival estimates along all possible routes and their values are weighted by route usage frequency, with expression used to calculate $S_{TCJ-CHP}$ is the most straightforward: $S_{TCJ-CHP}=  \Psi_{ORE} \cdot \theta_{HOR-CHP\,|\,ORE}$. The expression used to calculate $S_{HOR-CHP}$ includes more terms and is based on the same principle: $S_{HOR-CHP}=\Psi_{MAC} \cdot \theta_{TCJ-CHP\,|\,MAC} + \Psi_{TRN} \cdot \theta_{TCJ-CHP\,|\,TRN}$ and the expression for $S_{TCJ-CHP}$ is nested within it.
